Policies

Group Structure and Rewards

Groups structure themselves. Members share rewards equally. It's the group's responsibility to ensure adequate contribution. Document and report non-contributing members by April 4th to marjanh@hawaii.edu if necessary. Non-contributing members will be penalized.

Re-reads of Graded Coursework

Submit a brief written summary with your marked work for a re-read. Grades may go up or down upon re-read from scratch. Components are final one week after being returned. No personal lobbying; discussions are for educational purposes only.

Academic Dishonesty

Subject to UH Student Conduct Code. Includes cheating (unauthorized assistance, falsifying records, etc.) and plagiarism (submitting others' work as your own without attribution). Ignorance is no excuse.

AI Usage Policy

AI tools permitted for brainstorming, preliminary research, or proofreading if properly acknowledged. Students are responsible for originality and accuracy. Verify AI information through credible references. Relying too heavily on AI may undermine learning.

Safety

In case of a bomb threat/scare requiring evacuation, meet in the small street near the moped parking area on the Makai side of the building.

Disability Access

Contact KOKUA Program (956-7511, kokua@hawaii.edu, Student Services Center, Room 13) for confidential, free services.

Other

No recording or taping of the class without the instructor’s permission.
